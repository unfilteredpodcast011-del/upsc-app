1. Overview & Scope

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information necessary to provide exam practice sessions, maintain personal progress histories, and verify purchases:

  • Account Authentication Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Mobile OTP, we collect your verified name, email address, and profile identifier to authenticate your account.
  • Academic Preferences: Selected exam category (UPSC or TNPSC), preferred test language (English, Tamil, or Hindi), and target focus areas.
  • Exam Practice & Performance Telemetry: Test attempt timestamps, scorecards, accuracy rates, question answer selections, speed analytics, and downloaded evaluation reports.
  • Payment & Transaction Identifiers: When unlocking official practice packages (e.g. ₹1 fee), transactions are processed through RBI-authorized payment gateways (such as Razorpay). We receive and store confirmation tokens (Order ID, Payment ID) strictly to grant test access. We never store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

The Application requests only standard Android permissions that directly correlate with core learning features:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to fetch test packages, synchronize practice history, and download official exam question sets.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver daily test practice reminders, streak updates, and exam schedule announcements. You can toggle this permission off at any time in Android system settings.
  • STORAGE / DOWNLOAD ACCESS: Used strictly to save generated PDF scorecards and detailed trilingual solution sheets locally to your device.
No Sensitive Sensors: Test 11 does not request or access GPS location, camera, microphone, contact lists, or background location.

4. Multilingual Experience & Analytics

Test 11 provides learning materials in three languages: English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिन्दी). Language choices are saved locally on your device and synced with your student profile to maintain consistent test rendering across devices.

5. Data Security & Storage

All cloud interactions are transmitted over modern, encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. User profile records and session performance logs are hosted within secure, ISO/IEC 27001 certified cloud database infrastructure with strict row-level security (RLS) policies ensuring that only you have access to your personal test records.

6. Third-Party Services

The Application interfaces with trusted industry-standard service providers compliant with relevant data protection regulations:

  • Google Play Services: Core application distribution, in-app updates, and Google Sign-In.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud messaging for test notifications.
  • Supabase Cloud: Encrypted backend database for exam question storage and progress records.
  • Razorpay: PCI-DSS certified payment gateway for processing test unlocks.

7. Children's Privacy

Test 11 is built for young adults and higher education aspirants preparing for government competitive examinations. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

8. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership and rights over your personal data:

  • Access & Review: You can review your profile, registered mobile number, exam preferences, and test history directly within the app's Profile and History tabs.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated test records at any time by emailing us at plantgreeninertiamanagement@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Requests are honored and processed within 48 business hours.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory improvements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this public URL with an updated revision date.
